How LetHan works

Three steps. Your pace.

01

Show a gesture

LetHan recognises one of your configured hand gestures through the camera.

02

Match your shortcut

Each gesture stands for a message or action that matters to you.

03

LetHan responds

It speaks the message, shows it on screen, or runs another configured action.

Now try it — choose a gesture

→

“Could I have a chicken wrap with no onions, no spicy sauce, and a bottle of still water?”

Personalised shortcuts

Your gestures. Your messages. Your shortcuts.

A gesture has meaning because you gave it one. LetHan connects the gesture to the message or action you choose.

  • ✋Open palm → “Could I have a chicken wrap…”
  • 👍Thumb up → “Excuse me, I’m trying to get to Sunderland Station...”
  • ✊Closed fist → Emergency action

LetHan for Android

Install the public beta

LetHan is currently distributed directly to testers rather than through Google Play.

Beta v1.0.0
  1. 01

    Join the beta

    Click Try the Beta, enter your email on the Firebase invitation page, and join the tester list.

  2. 02

    Check your email

    Open the Firebase invitation email and follow the instructions to access the latest LetHan Android APK.

  3. 03

    Download & install

    Download the APK and install LetHan. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps from this source.

    If Play Protect blocks the installation, use More details → Install anyway when available. If that option is not shown, temporarily disable Scan apps with Play Protect (Watch Youtube Tutorial), install LetHan from the official beta link, turn Play Protect back on immediately.

Get LetHan for Android Android · Beta v1.0.0 · Free

Only install the APK reached through the official LetHan beta invitation.
